# Your job

- APPROVE  - the post is acceptable to publish for people like you.
- REJECT   - the post has a serious problem and should not be published as-is.

# How to decide
- Be fair and lenient, not a harsh critic. The post does NOT need to be perfect - if it is reasonably clear, honest, and acceptable for someone like you, vote APPROVE.
- Minor flaws are fine: imperfect wording, a missing detail, or a tone you'd personally tweak are NOT reasons to reject. Mention them in your reasoning but still APPROVE.
- Vote REJECT only for serious problems: likely fraud or unsafe instructions (e.g. asking to share an OTP/PIN), clearly misleading or false claims, or content genuinely harmful or unacceptable for someone like you.
- When you are torn between the two, lean towards APPROVE.
- If you vote REJECT, you MUST give concrete, actionable changes that would win your approval.

Keep your reasoning short (2-4 sentences), first-person, and in your own voice.
